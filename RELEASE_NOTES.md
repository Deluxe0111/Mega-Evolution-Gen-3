# Mega Evolution Gen 3 — v1.0.0-Beta

First versioned beta of Mega Evolution for Gen1Recomp's FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald runtime.

## Features

**Bring Mega Evolution to the original GBA adventures — without patching a ROM.**

- **98 Mega form entries, 92 Mega Stones and a Key Stone:** supported variant forms, including special requirements such as Mega Rayquaza's Dragon Ascent.
- **In-battle Mega controls:** toggle using **START**, the on-screen button or supported directional/mouse controls; select a move to evolve **before the attack**.
- **Temporary battle forms:** Mega base stats, compatible type and ability changes; one Mega Evolution per side per battle, persisting through switches and reverting when the Pokémon faints or battle ends.
- **New visual presentation:** animated emblem and white-silhouette transformation, normal/shiny front/back art, Mega HP-box indicator, party icons and cries.
- **Accessible Mega Stones:** lab aides in Oak's and Birch's laboratories sell the Key Stone and matching Mega Stones.
- **Mega-powered NPCs and bosses:** automatic eligible enemy Megas plus ROM-aware curated Gym Leaders, Elite Four, rival and villain fights in Kanto and Hoenn.
- **Trainer audit menu:** inspect boss party matches and export diagnostics from **START → MEGA AUDIT**.
- **Optional mod integrations:** National Dex Gen 3, G9 Battle Sprites, Gen 3 HD Sprites, Modern Spawns, Untamed Advanced and Nuzlocke. National Dex (or an equivalent compatible species provider) is required for post-Gen-3 species.


## Install

**Download the attached `mega-evolution-gen3-v1.0.0-Beta.zip`**, not the automatically generated GitHub source archive. Import it through Gen1Recomp's **MODS** screen and enable it for a Gen 3 game.

The base game does not require other mods. **National Dex Gen 3** is needed for later-generation Pokémon and corresponding Mega boss choices. G9 Battle Sprites, Gen 3 HD Sprites, Modern Spawns, Untamed Advanced and Nuzlocke are optional. See `README.md` and `CREDITS.md` inside the ZIP.

## Category and Beta limitations

Classified as a **Mechanics** mod in Gen1Recomp (`MECHANIC`), with no experimental enablement warning.

This is a testing pre-release. FireRed/Emerald live verification and the complete five-game/cross-mod compatibility matrix are unfinished. Wally's Emerald curated encounter remains audit-only until his ROM records are confirmed. Modern Fairy/ability/move interactions depend on the installed provider, and compatibility may vary.

The repository is private; **third-party sprite, UI and audio redistribution rights must be verified before any wider public distribution**. No ROM or copyrighted game binary is included. Artist/source credits are bundled with the package.

Verify your download with the attached `.sha256` file.
